# C05 Sources

All three sounds come from Freesound and are used in every piece. The low and mid are two kicks pitched differently in the patch; the high is a closed hi-hat.

## C05-S01-steady-pulse_FAV.wav
- Low: "Kick with low end 2" by evilc00kie, https://freesound.org/people/evilc00kie/sounds/517551/, CC0
- Mid: "03 kick1.wav" by meshgarden, https://freesound.org/people/meshgarden/sounds/333677/, CC0
- High: "rd-6 c hihat.wav" by laffik, https://freesound.org/people/laffik/sounds/638084/, CC0
- Settings: pitch 0.6 / 1.2 / 1; probability 0 / 0 / 0.6
